## About — `/about`

Company introduction, the same experience stats as the homepage trust block,
and the philosophy statement:

> We do not fill roof space with solar modules. We engineer, analyse and
> design solar plants to perform for 25–30 years.

## Engineering — `/engineering`

Four capability groups, detailed in [Components](components.md): pre-
construction engineering, detailed execution engineering, procurement and
quality, advanced EPC.

## Services — `/services`

Four service cards: Residential Rooftop Solar, Commercial Solar, Industrial
Solar, Solar System Maintenance. Full scope per card in
[Components](components.md).

## Projects — `/projects`

Filterable portfolio grid plus a project detail view. Everything about what
can be shown and when lives in its own page —
[Project portfolio](projects.md) — because publication permission is a
governance rule, not just a UI feature.

## SolarCare — `/solarcare`

Four AMC tiers — Essential (1 year), Plus (3 years), Premium (5 years), Elite
(10 years). No pricing goes on this page until the client confirms it.

## Government Schemes — `/schemes`

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana: eligibility guidance, documentation support,
installation, application assistance, net metering. This page carries the
subsidy disclaimer — see [SEO](seo.md) and
[Client confirmations](client_confirmations.md); no subsidy amount is
hardcoded here or anywhere else on the site.
