# 01 — Overview & Project Understanding

## Client Positioning

Fivefold Renewable Pvt. Ltd. is an engineering-led solar EPC company with:

- 10+ years of renewable energy experience
- 20+ MW of industrial and commercial rooftop solar installations
- 30+ industrial and institutional projects
- 800+ MW of cumulative design, engineering and consultation experience

The website must reflect this positioning. It is **not** a conventional informational brochure site.

## Website Vision

> **From a Solar Website to a Solar Decision Platform**

The platform should allow visitors to:

1. Understand Fivefold’s solar solutions
2. Explore residential, commercial and industrial offerings
3. Discover applicable government schemes
4. Estimate their potential solar requirements
5. Find suitable solutions based on their inputs
6. Explore projects and credentials
7. Understand AMC, O&M and warranty support
8. Submit enquiries and generate qualified leads

## Design Philosophy

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Clean** | Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, no visual clutter |
| **Interactive** | Calculator, scheme finder, filters and contextual CTAs drive engagement |
| **Minimal** | Only essential pages and elements. One primary action per screen |
| **Conversion-focused** | Every interactive path ends in a clear lead-capture moment |

## Core Message

**“Powering Odisha with Smarter Solar Energy”**  
Supporting line: Engineering-Led Solar EPC Solutions Since 2016

The site should feel premium, engineering-driven and trustworthy — never salesy or overloaded.
