## 3. Content Restrictions & Compliance Guidelines

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Content Rules:**
> 1. **No Unsupported Tax Claims:** Do NOT use claims like `"40% Accelerated Depreciation"` on the homepage or promotional materials unless explicitly verified, context-qualified, and client-approved.
> 2. **No Guaranteed Returns:** Financial savings, ROI figures, subsidy amounts, and tax benefits must never be presented as "guaranteed". Focus on engineering reliability, system performance, and long-term operation.
> 3. **Keep Homepage Concise:** The homepage slider is an entry point. Detailed specifications, full application lists, and technical parameters belong on dedicated solution pages.
